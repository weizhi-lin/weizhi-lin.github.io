## Twin Intelligence Lab Overview

<p align="center">
  <img src="/assets/img/DTlab.png" alt="Twin Intelligence Lab Flyer" style="max-width:100%; height:auto;">
</p>

- Our goal is to connect real systems, real data, and real-world impact. 
- We work on problems where engineering and physical systems generate noisy, incomplete, and multimodal data, such as sensor signals, images, and complex geometric data.

### Research Focus

- Multimodal data from sensors, images, and operational systems
- Machine learning and statistical modeling for noisy and imperfect real-world data
- Design of experiments, prediction, anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis
- Decision support for smart manufacturing and healthcare systems

### Application Areas

- Smart manufacturing: 3D printing qualification, quality control, and process monitoring
- Smart healthcare: biosensing, monitoring, and treatment-planning support
- Embodied AI and robotics
